KenshiCoop - play Kenshi together
=================================

This is the co-op mod for Kenshi. It is one file, KenshiCoop-Setup.exe, and it
sets everything up for you. It takes about 2 minutes.

BEFORE YOU START
  - Kenshi installed on Steam
  - Steam open and signed in
  - Kenshi CLOSED


INSTALLING
----------
1. Download KenshiCoop-Setup.exe.
   If your browser or Google Drive warns that it isn't commonly downloaded or
   can't be scanned, choose "Keep" or "Download anyway".

2. Double-click it.
   Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC". That's only
   because the file is new and unsigned, not because anything is wrong.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   If your antivirus blocks it, let me know what it says.

3. A small "KenshiCoop Setup" window opens. It should say "Kenshi found:"
   with your Kenshi folder.

4. Click "Install". It backs up your saves first (about a minute), then
   installs everything. When it says "KenshiCoop is installed", click OK and
   close the window.


CHECKING IT WORKED
------------------
5. Start Kenshi from Steam like normal, and click OK in the little Kenshi
   launcher window.

6. On the main menu, look at the bottom-right corner. It should say:
       RE_Kenshi 0.3.5 - Kenshi 1.0.65

Please send me back:
  - a screenshot of the setup window after it finishes (the text in the box)
  - a screenshot of the main menu showing that bottom-right text


PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
When we're ready, start Kenshi and wait at the main menu.
Press F2, type your name, choose "Client" and "Steam", paste the Steam ID I
send you, and click "Connect". You'll get my world automatically - no need to
load a save.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Screenshot whatever message you see and send it to me. Nothing in your game is
changed if the setup stops partway.
Your save backup is in:  Documents > KenshiCoop backups


TO UNDO IT
----------
Run KenshiCoop-Setup.exe again and click "Uninstall".
